# GTM 4.4.1 Public Beta: Game-by-game verification

**This is a maintainer-reported Steam Deck acceptance summary, not exhaustive hardware certification.** The Arcade remains experimental, and the core GTM product is music + visualizers.

| Interactive visualizer | Game type | Current status | Key qualifications |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Neon Paddle Championship** | Pong-inspired paddle competition | **Verified on real Steam Deck**, including multiplayer | Human-vs-AI and AI-vs-AI work; Chaos, Morph and Mindball are experimental; occasional brief human-to-AI transition stutter remains. |
| **Orbital Duel** | Ship/base tactical combat | **Verified on real Steam Deck**, including multiplayer | Not a claim of fully balanced AI or finished gameplay depth. |
| **Orbit Defenders** | Space-invaders-inspired arcade survival/defense | **Verified on real Steam Deck**, including multiplayer | Boss variety and deeper upgrades remain potential experiments, not commitments. |
| **Neon Strike Gallery** | Target/accuracy gallery shooter | **Reported working** | Multiplayer was mentioned tentatively, not independently confirmed. |
| **Beat Catcher** | Catcher + experimental four-lane rhythm game | **Included; physical acceptance incomplete** | Piano Lanes, taps and long notes exercised in automated tests; full hardware testing not claimed. |
| **Neon Chess Championship** | Chess | **VERY EARLY ALPHA** | Enlarged board now visually works on Deck. The game is bare-bones and is NOT accepted as a complete, polished or fully working chess experience. May be removed. |

## What else works?

The maintainer reports that **the original GTM visualizers and their navigation work on Steam Deck**. Older real hardware images are available in the [Visual Worlds gallery](SCREENSHOTS.md).

The original music/ambient/store features and ResonaDeck compatibility were accepted in earlier GTM versions; those past tests aren't a formal full retest of every 4.4.1 combination.

## Why a public beta?

We want to learn whether interactive music visualizers enhance GTM or distract from its main purpose. **Nothing in this beta means GTM has committed to becoming a games launcher.** Priorities and the lineup may change.

**[Leave Arcade feedback, request changes, or suggest removing Chess](https://github.com/ValentineBSAA/Game-Theme-Music/issues/4)**.
