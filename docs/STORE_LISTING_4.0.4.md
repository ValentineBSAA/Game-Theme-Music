# 🎵 GTM 4.0.4 | Visual Worlds Public Beta

A major Steam Deck music overhaul: smoother automatic **8-second crossfade**, **22 reactive visualizers across four categories**, **unlimited music playlists**, optional **Shuffle All Visualizers**, and a safe **Visualizer Pack Manager** for adding custom collections without reinstalling GTM.

New scenes include CRT Pulse Field, Liquid Chrome, Disc Prism XL, Beat Pinball, Neon Pong, Seismic Mountains, Mechanical Iris, Chromatic Event Horizon, Sakura Tempest and Tidal Bloom. Improved footer blending and music-reactive colors are built for Gaming Mode. Native Steam Store and Decky Plugin Store share one Store Music playlist. ResonaDeck audio focus remains compatible, and a passive telemetry bridge prepares for future Plex visualization (not yet functional end to end).
