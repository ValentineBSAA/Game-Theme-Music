# Game Theme Music 3.7.0 | Mini Player HUD control

You asked, we listened. 🎵

New in 3.7.0: **Show mini player HUD** under **Settings → Interface & Feedback**.

- **ON (default):** Enjoy the existing animated Now Playing mini player.
- **OFF:** Keep your game pages clean while music continues uninterrupted.
- Works across Steam and non-Steam games, Ambient Home music, Steam Store, and Plugin Store.
- Preference survives restarts; switching it back on restores the mini player.
- No changes to music playback, playlists, controller controls, or the ResonaDeck audio focus bridge.

**Steam Deck hardware-tested** on Catherine Classic, Need for Speed: Most Wanted (2005), Steam Store, Plugin Store, and Home Ambient music.

Thanks to **Dollet** for raising this usability request in our Discord!
