# Game Theme Music 3.7.0 — Mini Player HUD preference

3.7.0 adds the user-requested **Show mini player HUD** setting under Settings → Interface & feedback. Keep the SAO-style Now Playing HUD, or switch it OFF without silencing game music, Ambient, Store playlists, or track controls.

The setting defaults to ON for compatibility with previous GTM releases. When switched OFF, active ribbons disappear and queued popups are discarded, including when navigating in/out of Quick Access. The HUD stays OFF after a reload. Turn it back ON to see future playback events. All existing 3.6.9 features remain.

Thanks to Dollet for reporting this UX gap through the GTM Discord bug-reports channel.
