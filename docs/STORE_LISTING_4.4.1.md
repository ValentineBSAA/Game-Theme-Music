# 🎵 Game Theme Music 4.4.1 | Public Beta

**Music for your Steam Deck. Visual Worlds that react. An optional Arcade experiment.**

GTM brings game-specific soundtracks, Home Ambient, Steam/Decky Store music, playlists, crossfade, customizable reactive visualizers and optional ResonaDeck visualization into Steam Deck Gaming Mode.

### What's new in the 4.4.x public beta?
- Improved nested visualizer navigation with readable, accessible page controls instead of a dead-end **More Choices** item.
- Optional **Neon Paddle Championship**, plus experimental **Orbital Duel, Orbit Defenders, Neon Strike Gallery, Beat Catcher** and **Neon Chess Championship**.
- **4.4.1** enlarges Chess's focused board view for Couch / TV while keeping Steam Home visible.
- Steam Deck maintainer testing confirms Pong, Orbital Duel and Orbit Defenders multiplayer. Neon Strike is reported working; Beat Catcher isn't completely hardware-verified.
- **Chess is VERY EARLY ALPHA**, not a finished experience. Brief Pong AI/controller handoff stutters may remain.

**Arcade games are visualizer experiments, not GTM's new permanent direction.** We welcome feedback on which belong here.
