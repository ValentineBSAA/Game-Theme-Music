# 🎵 GTM community ideas and feature requests

GTM is a **music-first Steam Deck plugin** maintained by Valentine. **4.5.8 is feature-complete for its current direction and in active maintenance.** The priority is fixing bugs, preventing regressions, supporting SteamOS/Decky changes and improving reliability, not adding a constant stream of new features. The project is **not abandoned**. [Read the maintenance policy](MAINTENANCE.md). In the current **4.5.8** release, Arcade-themed visualizers are **automatic AI spectator scenes**, not human-playable games. The playable Arcade and early-alpha Chess described in older 4.4.1 notes are historical experiments, not active 4.5.8 features.

## What feedback helps?

Describe what you're trying to do on your Steam Deck, what happened and what you'd rather see. Bug reports and compatibility observations involving visualizers, color palettes, accessibility, controller navigation, soundtrack playback, playlists, Ambient/Store playback, ResonaDeck cooperation and performance are especially useful. You may still suggest an idea, but major expansion requests will normally be deferred or marked Not Planned during maintenance.

For spectator scenes, mention which visualizer, the observed AI behavior, power effects, ball physics mode, selected layout and whether a different CSS Loader theme is in use. Provide a screenshot or clip if convenient, **not because it is required**. Avoid including account tokens, private files or logs with personal details.

## How requests are evaluated

Look for existing ideas first; supporting a related issue is helpful. If yours is different, open a new request. Proposed changes are judged by usefulness, technical feasibility, Steam Deck performance, compatibility and maintenance cost. An issue may be marked Suggested, Under Review, Accepted, In Development, Preview Testing, Released or Not Planned. During maintenance, **confirmed regressions and compatibility issues come first**; no new major features are scheduled. **Votes are signals, not promises or deadlines.**

The earlier feature-priority poll used reactions on individual option comments. It is now **archived as an earlier planning discussion**, not an invitation to assume new visualizer or Arcade features are coming. GitHub Discussions is not enabled for this repository, so Issues remains the supported feedback channel.
